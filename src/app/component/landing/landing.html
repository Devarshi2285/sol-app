Features

Why SolSign?

Enterprise-grade document signing powered by blockchain technology.

On-Chain Security

Every signature is recorded as an immutable transaction on the Solana blockchain. No tampering. No disputes.

Multi-Party Signing

Add multiple contributors to any document. Track who has signed and who is pending in real time.

Transparent Logs

Complete activity history for every document. Every action is logged with the signer's verified identity.

How It Works

Three Simple Steps

From profile creation to signed documents — in minutes.

01

Create Your Profile

Register your on-chain identity with a username. Your wallet becomes your verified signature.

02

Create a Document

Link any document and add wallet addresses of contributors who need to sign it.

03

Contributors Sign

Each contributor signs with their wallet. Once all sign, the document is finalized on-chain forever.
